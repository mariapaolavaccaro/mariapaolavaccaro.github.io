[Read the article](https://structures.uni-heidelberg.de/blog/posts/2025_03/index.php)

**Girls' Day "Physik hautnah!"** — I co-organize an annual workshop, *A dance of stars and black holes*, for school-aged students as part of the national Girls' Day programme (2024, 2025, 2026).

**Gist.Science** — My paper on the formation of binary black holes in AGN disks was featured on [Gist.Science](https://gist.science/paper/2508.03637#gist), a platform that makes research accessible to a broad audience. [Read the summary](https://gist.science/paper/2508.03637#gist)

**Scholé** — I am a member of the organizing committee of [Scholé](https://www.filosofiaroccella.it/), a nonprofit that brings physics, philosophy, and history to the general public through weekly seminars and an annual Summer School in Calabria, Italy. If you speak Italian, you are welcome to join — sessions are open to everyone, in person or online.
