## Research profile

I am a computational astrophysicist and PhD candidate at Heidelberg University (supervisor: Prof. Michela Mapelli), modelling how black holes grow from stellar to intermediate and supermassive masses, and what their gravitational-wave mergers reveal about the early Universe. I showed that AGN disks can grow black holes through the pair-instability gap into the intermediate-mass range, but only in low-viscosity disks with efficient gas hardening, and that the resulting populations match systems like GW190521 and GW231123. Expected defence January 2028; available for postdoc and fellowship positions from late 2027.
