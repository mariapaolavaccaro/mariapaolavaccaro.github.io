excerpt: 'We explore the impact of gas accretion and migration traps on BBH evolution in AGN disks using a new fast semi-analytic model. Efficient gas hardening greatly enhances hierarchical mergers, leading to intermediate-mass BH formation and spin alignment, with observable signatures in the mass-ratio/effective-spin anticorrelation.'
