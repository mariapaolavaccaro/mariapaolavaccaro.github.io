M. Paola Vaccaro

PhD Student in Astrophysics  ·  ITA/ZAH, Heidelberg University

I am a computational astrophysicist modelling how black holes grow from stellar to intermediate and supermassive masses, and what their gravitational-wave mergers reveal about the early Universe. I showed that AGN disks can grow black holes through the pair-instability gap into the intermediate-mass range, but only in low-viscosity disks with efficient gas hardening, and that the resulting populations match systems like GW190521 and GW231123.

Intermediate-mass black holes · AGN disk dynamics · star cluster dynamics · gravitational-wave sources · stellar collisions · Einstein Telescope science
